# 🤖 Starter Kit

A beginner-friendly **Robotics Starter Kit** designed to introduce students to the fundamentals of **electronics, circuits, components, and basic robotics** through hands-on activities.

The kit includes essential electronic components and a set of practical projects that help students learn how circuits work, build confidence with electronics, and develop basic troubleshooting and problem-solving skills.

## 📚 What's Included?

The kit contains:

* 💡 LEDs & RGB LED
* 🔊 Buzzer
* 📳 Vibrator Motor
* 🔌 Transistors & Diodes
* ⚡ Capacitors
* 🎛️ Potentiometer
* 🔘 Push Buttons
* 🔀 Slide, Toggle & Rocker Switches
* 🔋 Batteries & Battery Clip
* 🧵 Jumper Wires
* 🟫 Breadboard
* 🟤 Conductive Copper Tape
* 🟨 Resistors
* 🪙 Coin Cell Batteries

## 🔬 Projects

The manual includes **12 hands-on electronics activities** covering:

1. 💡 LED Glow
2. 💡 LED Glow with Paper Circuit
3. 🔘 LED Glow with Switch
4. 🔊 Alarm with Switch
5. 🚦 Traffic Light Using LEDs
6. 🔗 Series Lights Using Paper Circuit
7. 🔗 Parallel Lights Using Paper Circuit
8. 💡 Series LED Circuit
9. 💡 Parallel LED Circuit
10. ⏱️ LED with Capacitor & Transistor
11. 🎛️ Adjustable LED with Potentiometer
12. 📏 Working with a Multimeter

These projects introduce students to **basic circuit building, series and parallel connections, switching, paper circuits, transistor operation, capacitors, variable resistance, and electrical measurements**.

## 🛡️ Safety

Always check circuit connections and component ratings before applying power.

* Do not short-circuit batteries.
* Check LED and battery polarity.
* Never measure resistance on a powered circuit.
* Connect a multimeter **in series** when measuring current.
* Handle electronic components and tools carefully.
